                        FormCut

                        Instead of CapCut

                        APACHE-2.0MACOS · WINDOWS · LINUX

                        Release being prepared
                        Other versions

                        FormCut is a complete video editor that runs entirely on your machine, with no account and no upload. A multi-track timeline, one renderer shared by preview and export, a catalogue of grades, effects and transitions, captions and subject cutout from local models, and a scripting API come in a desktop shell. Claude Code, Codex or Cursor can build, render and collect an edit over MCP.

                        What it does

                        • Previews and exports through one renderer, so the frame you approve is the frame you ship, held by a golden-frame check in CI.
                        • Browses 74 colour grades, 76 visual effects, 48 audio chains, 23 transitions and a library of 180 looks.
                        • Transcribes with Whisper locally, removes backgrounds with models that ship in the engine and tracks subjects, planes and rotoscoped shapes.
                        • Exports H.264, HEVC, AV1 and VP9 with hardware encoders, and exchanges timelines as OpenTimelineIO, EDL and Final Cut XML.

                        FormVis

                        Instead of Photoshop

                        APACHE-2.0MACOS · WINDOWS · LINUX

                        Release being prepared
                        Other versions

                        FormVis is a layered image editor: stack photos and paintings as layers, group them in folders, mask and clip them, blend them, select, paint and export. It saves its own .fvd projects, opens layered PSD and PSB files, and runs in a browser or as a desktop app for macOS, Linux and Windows. Selection, background removal, fill and upscaling run on local models.

                        What it does

                        • Composites layers with pass-through folders, 27 blend modes, raster and folder masks, clipping chains and fill layers.
