# FormPortal

FormPortal is the Form family's app portal: one window that installs, opens, updates, rolls back and removes every Form app, with betas beside the stable builds and an open extension index. No account and no background process.

- [Getting Started](Getting-Started)
- [Architecture](Architecture)
- [Agents](Agents)
- [Releasing](Releasing)

FormPortal reads the catalogue formcompiler (`nodemode-dev/formcompiler`) publishes beside every Form app's releases. The apps themselves live in their own repositories and stay downloadable on their own; FormPortal is never required to run one.
