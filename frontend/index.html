Every Form app. One portal.

FORM APPS

Photo, image, layout, vector, motion, video, sound, notes, slides and code. Free, open-source apps that run on your machine, installed and kept current from here.

Beta

Builds that are still being tested. A beta installs beside the stable build with its own settings, and it can be unstable.

Extensions

Effects, looks, templates and plugins from the open catalogue. Each one says who made it, what it is for and what it can use.

Each package is checked against the catalogue before it is installed. An app uses its extensions from the next time it starts. To install a .formpkg file, open it with FormPortal.
