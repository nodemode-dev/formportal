Apps

  • formcut

    Video editing that stays on your machine

    A multi-track timeline, colour grading with scopes, captions and speech from local models, and an export that matches the preview, because both run through one renderer.

  • formvis

    Layered image editing, with your Photoshop files

    Layers, masks and blend modes, forty-five tools, RAW development and colour management, and local models for selection, fill and upscaling.

  • formwav

    Audio editing where what plays is what renders

    Record, edit, repair and mix on one mixer shared by playback and export, and edit speech by editing its transcript.
